Data Governance

Privacy Policy & Confidentiality Terms

Last Revised: October 2026 • Headquarters: Msheireb Downtown, Doha, State of Qatar

1. Institutional Commitment to Privacy

AR Investor's ("we", "us", "our") operates as a specialized private capital and strategic advisory firm headquartered in Msheireb Downtown, Doha, State of Qatar. We are dedicated to maintaining strict confidentiality, professional discretion, and rigorous security over all personal and corporate information entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy governs our processing of personal data gathered via our official digital portal (https://arinvestors.in), direct electronic communications, and institutional mandate inquiries, adhering to State of Qatar Law No. 13 of 2016 concerning Personal Data Privacy Protection (PDPPL) and international data governance frameworks.

2. Scope of Collected Information

We collect information solely to facilitate institutional dialogue and evaluate potential mandates:

  • Corporate & Contact Credentials: Name, professional title, corporate affiliation, institutional email address, and transaction parameters submitted via our inquiry interface.
  • Communication Records: Correspondence exchanged through our official communications desk at info@arinvestors.in.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized network logs, browser agent signatures, and timestamp data gathered strictly to maintain system integrity and prevent malicious intrusion.

We do not harvest consumer tracking profiles, purchase behavioral profiles, or monetize user data in any manner.

3. Legal Grounds & Operational Utilization

All information collected is processed on the following lawful bases:

  • Legitimate Professional Interest: Reviewing incoming transaction teasers, strategic restructuring briefs, and maritime asset acquisition proposals.
  • Pre-Contractual Due Diligence: Conducting preliminary Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know-Your-Customer (KYC) screening prior to formal non-disclosure agreement (NDA) execution.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Fulfilling reporting, auditing, and corporate governance requirements mandated under Qatari and international commercial law.

4. Absolute Confidentiality & Zero Data Commercialization

AR Investor's will never sell, lease, license, or commercially distribute client or visitor data to third-party marketing brokers. Data is accessible solely to authorized principals, compliance counsel, and secure enterprise infrastructure providers bound by enforceable non-disclosure agreements.

5. Cookies and Telemetry Policy

Our website does not deploy invasive commercial tracking pixels or cross-site tracking cookies. We utilize only essential session memory and anonymized performance telemetry required for responsive delivery and security firewall verification.

6. Rights of Data Subjects

Under applicable data protection legislation, you possess the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether your personal corporate data is retained by AR Investor's.
  • Obtain a copy of stored records subject to statutory confidentiality limitations.
  • Request immediate correction of inaccurate data or complete erasure of historical correspondence.

To exercise these rights, direct a verified corporate request to our governance team at: info@arinvestors.in.
