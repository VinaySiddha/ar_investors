Privacy policy

Last revised October 2026 · Msheireb Downtown, Doha, State of Qatar

1. Our commitment

AR Investor’s is a strategic advisory firm headquartered in Msheireb Downtown, Doha, Qatar. This policy covers personal data collected through arinvestors.in and direct communications, in line with Qatar Law No. 13 of 2016 on Personal Data Privacy Protection and applicable international frameworks.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, title, organisation, work email and the content of your enquiry.
  • Correspondence sent to our advisory desk.
  • Technical data: anonymised logs and browser details used to keep the site secure.

We do not build consumer tracking profiles or monetise personal data.

3. Why we use it

  • Legitimate professional interest in reviewing proposals and enquiries.
  • Pre-contract due diligence, including AML and KYC screening before any NDA.
  • Meeting legal, audit and governance obligations.

4. Confidentiality

We never sell, lease or license client or visitor data to marketers. Access is limited to authorised principals, compliance counsel and infrastructure providers bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Cookies

The site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Only essential technical storage and anonymised performance data are used.

6. Your rights

You may ask whether we hold your data, request a copy, and ask for correction or deletion, subject to legal limits. Email info@arinvestors.in from your work address.
